Features

Everything you need to automate voids

Built specifically for MEP professionals who value precision and efficiency.

Smart Intersection Detection

Automatically detects pipes, ducts, and cable trays intersecting with walls and structural frames using solid boolean operations.

Rectangular & Round Voids

Choose between rectangular and round void shapes. Configure offsets, approximation steps, and discipline labels to match your project standards.

Linked Document Support

Detects intersections across both the active document and all linked Revit models. No need to bind links first.

Revit 2021 – 2026

Supports .NET Framework 4.8 (Revit 2021–2024) and .NET 8.0 (Revit 2025–2026). Seamless integration with your BIM workflow.

One-Click Execution

Select your elements, click execute. Voids are placed instantly with correct sizing and positioning. It's that simple.

Combine Voids

Merge two or more overlapping void markings into a single, correctly sized opening with one click.
